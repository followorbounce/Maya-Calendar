How the Cycles Nest

The Long Count is a place-value, mostly base-20 (vigesimal) system — with one irregular step, since 18 uinals make a tun, so that a tun approximates a 360-day count rather than a full 400.

Directional cosmology

Four Directions, One Center

Colonial and epigraphic sources associate the cardinal directions with specific colors, most explicitly in the Postclassic Dresden and Madrid Codices and in the colonial Books of Chilam Balam.

Editorial note — this reference distinguishes primary epigraphic/iconographic attestation from colonial-era systematization (much of the tidy four-direction/four-color scheme as usually presented derives from colonial Yucatec sources like the Chilam Balam and from Landa, and its uniform application to the Classic period is a scholarly extrapolation, not a direct Classic-era statement).
